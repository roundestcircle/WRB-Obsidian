#Properties

## Diagnostic Criteria

Continuous rock (from Latin continuare, to continue) is consolidated material, exclusive of cemented pedogenic horizons such as [[Limonic Horizon|limonic]], [[Petrocalcic Horizon|petrocalcic]], [[Petroduric Horizon|petroduric]], [[Petrogypsic Horizon|petrogypsic]], [[Petroplinthic Horizon|petroplinthic]] and [[Spodic Horizon|spodic]] horizons. Continuous rock is sufficiently consolidated to remain intact when an air-dried specimen, 25–30 mm on one side, is submerged in water for 1 hour. The material is considered continuous only if cracks occupy < 10% (by volume, related to the whole soil) of the continuous rock, with no significant displacement of the rock having taken place.

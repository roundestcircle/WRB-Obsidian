#Soils

## Key to the Reference Group

Other soils having:

1. a *cambic horizon*
   a. starting ≤ 50 cm from the mineral soil surface; *and*
   b. having its lower limit ≥ 25 cm from the mineral soil surface;
   *or*
2. an *anthraquic, hydragric, irrigagic, plaggic, pretic or terric horizon*;
   *or*
3. a *fragic, thionic* or *vertic horizon* starting ≤ 100 cm from the mineral soil surface;
   *or*
4. a *tsitelic horizon* with a texture class of sandy loam or finer, starting ≤ 50 cm from the mineral soil surface;
   *or*
5. one or more layers with *andic* or *vitric properties* with a combined thickness of ≥ 15 cm within 100 cm of the soil surface.

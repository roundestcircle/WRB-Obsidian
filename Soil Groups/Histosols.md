#Soils

## Key to the Reference Group

Soils having one or more of the following:

1. [[Organic Material]] starting ≤ 40 cm from the soil surface and having within 100 cm of the soil surface a combined thickness of:
   - ≥ 40 cm if < 75% (by volume, related to the fine earth plus all dead plant residues) consists of moss fibres; *or*
   - ≥ 60 cm;
   *or*
1. organic material starting at the soil surface, having a thickness of ≥ 10 cm and directly overlying ice, continuous rock or [[Technic Hard Material]]
   *or*
2. a layer of coarse fragments that, together with overlying organic material, if present, starts at the soil surface and has a thickness of
   - ≥ 10 cm if overlying continuous rock or technic hard material; *or*
   - ≥ 40 cm;
   *and* the major part of the interstices between the coarse fragments is filled with organic material and the remaining interstices, if present, are void.

#Soils

## Key to the Reference Group

Other soils having:

1. a [[Chernic Horizon]]; *and*
2. starting ≤ 50 cm below the lower limit of the [[Mollic Horizon]] and, if present, above a [[Petrocalcic Horizon]], a layer with [[Protocalcic Properties]], ≥ 5 cm thick, or a [[Calcic Horizon]]; *and*
3. a base saturation (by 1 M NH<sub>4</sub>OAc, pH 7) of ≥ 50% from the mineral soil surface to the layer with protocalcic properties or to the calcic horizon, throughout.

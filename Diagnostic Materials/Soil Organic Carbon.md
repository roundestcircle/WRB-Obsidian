#Materials

## Diagnostic Criteria

Soil organic carbon (from Greek *organon*, tool, and Latin *carbo*, coal) is organic carbon that does not meet the set of diagnostic criteria of [[Artefacts|artefacts]].

## Relationships with some other Diagnostics

For organic carbon meeting the criteria of [[Artefacts|artefacts]], the [[Garbic]] or the [[Carbonic]] qualifier may apply.

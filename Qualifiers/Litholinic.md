#Qualifier
Abbreviation:: lh

Litholinic (lh) (from Greek lithos, stone, and Latin linea, line): having a layer, ≥ 2 and ≤ 20 cm thick and starting ≤ 150 cm from the mineral soil surface, that has ≥ 40% (by volume, related to the whole soil) coarse fragments and in the layers above and below < 10% (by volume, related to the whole soil) coarse fragments (stone line) (1, referring to the upper limit of the layer).

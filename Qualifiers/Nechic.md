Abbreviation:: ne

Nechic (ne) (from Amharic nech, white): having a pHwater of < 5 and uncoated mineral grains of sand and/or
coarse silt size in a darker matrix somewhere within 5 cm of the mineral soil surface and no spodic
horizon starting ≤ 200 cm from the mineral soil surface.
Neobrunic (nb): see Brunic
Neocambic (nc): see Cambic.

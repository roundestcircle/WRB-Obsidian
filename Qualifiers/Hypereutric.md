#Qualifier
Abbreviation:: je Maingroup:: Eutric

Hypereutric (je) (from Greek hyper, over):
• in [[Histosols]], having a pHwater of ≥ 5.5 throughout in the [[Organic Material|organic material]] within 100 cm of the soil surface and ≥ 6.5 in the major part with organic material within 100 cm of the soil surface,
• in other soils, having [[Mineral Material|mineral material]], throughout: ➢ from 20 to 100 cm of the mineral soil surface, or ➢ from 20 cm of the mineral soil surface to a limiting layer starting ≥ 50 cm from the mineral soil surface, whichever is shallower, that has exchangeable (Ca+Mg+K+Na) ≥ exchangeable Al; and in its major part exchangeable (Ca+Mg+K+Na) ≥ 4 times the exchangeable Al.

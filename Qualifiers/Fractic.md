Abbreviation:: fc
Subgroups:: [[Calcifractic]], [[Gypsofractic]]

Fractic (fc) (from Latin fractus, broken): having a layer, ≥ 10 cm thick and starting ≤ 100 cm from the
mineral soil surface, consisting of a broken-up petrocalcic or petrogypsic horizon, the remnants of which:
• occupy ≥ 40% (by volume, related to the whole soil), and
• have an average horizontal length of < 10 cm and/or occupy < 80% (by volume, related to the whole
soil) (2).

Abbreviation:: iy
Maingroup:: [[Gleyic]]

Inclinigleyic (iy) (from Latin inclinare, to bow): having a layer, ≥ 25 cm thick and starting ≤ 75 cm
from the mineral soil surface, that has gleyic properties throughout and reducing conditions in some
parts of every sublayer; and having a slope inclination of ≥ 5% and a subsurface water flow for some
time during the year (2).

#Qualifier
Abbreviation:: iy Maingroup:: Gleyic

Inclinigleyic (iy) (from Latin inclinare, to bow): having a layer, ≥ 25 cm thick and starting ≤ 75 cm from the mineral soil surface, that has [[Gleyic Properties|gleyic properties]] throughout and [[Reducing Conditions|reducing conditions]] in some parts of every sublayer; and having a slope inclination of ≥ 5% and a subsurface water flow for some time during the year (2).

Abbreviation:: hy

Hydric (hy) (from Greek hydor, water): having within 100 cm of the soil surface one or more layers with a
combined thickness of ≥ 35 cm that have andic properties and a water content ≥ 70% (mass of water
divided by mass of dry soil) at 1500 kPa tension, measured without previous drying of the sample (in
Andosols only) (2).

Abbreviation:: sp
Subgroups:: [[Hyperspolic]]

Spolic (sp) (from Latin spoliare, to exploit): having a layer, ≥ 20 cm thick and within 100 cm of the soil
surface, with ≥ 20% (by volume, weighted average, related to the whole soil) artefacts, ≥ 35% of which
consist of industrial products (e.g. mine spoil, dredgings, slag, ash, rubble, etc.) (in Technosols only) (2).

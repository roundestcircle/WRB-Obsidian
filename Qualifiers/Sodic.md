#Qualifier
Abbreviation:: so Subgroups:: Argisodic, Protosodic

Sodic (so) (from Arabic suda, headache - referring to the headache-alleviating properties of sodium carbonate): having a layer, ≥ 20 cm thick and starting ≤ 100 cm from the mineral soil surface, that has ≥ 15% Na plus Mg and ≥ 6% Na on the exchange complex; and not having a [[Natric Horizon|natric horizon]] starting ≤ 100 cm from the soil surface (2).

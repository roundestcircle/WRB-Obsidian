#Qualifier
Abbreviation:: ub Subgroups:: Hyperurbic

Urbic (ub) (from Latin urbs, city): having a layer, ≥ 20 cm thick and within 100 cm of the soil surface, with ≥ 20% (by volume, weighted average, related to the whole soil) [[Artefacts|artefacts]], ≥ 35% of which consist of rubble and refuse of human settlements (in [[Technosols]] only) (2).

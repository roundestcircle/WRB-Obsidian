Abbreviation:: gl
Subgroups:: [[Inclinigleyic]], [[Oxygleyic]], [[Protogleyic]], [[Relictigleyic]]

Gleyic (gl) (from Russian folk name gley, wet bluish clay): having a layer, ≥ 25 cm thick and starting
≤ 75 cm from the mineral soil surface, that has gleyic properties throughout and reducing conditions in
some parts of every sublayer (2).

#Qualifier
Abbreviation:: gl Subgroups:: Inclinigleyic, Oxygleyic, Protogleyic, Relictigleyic

Gleyic (gl) (from Russian folk name gley, wet bluish clay): having a layer, ≥ 25 cm thick and starting ≤ 75 cm from the mineral soil surface, that has [[Gleyic Properties|gleyic properties]] throughout and [[Reducing Conditions|reducing conditions]] in some parts of every sublayer (2).

#Qualifier
Abbreviation:: rl Maingroup:: Gleyic

Relictigleyic (rl) (from Latin relictus, left back): having a layer, ≥ 25 cm thick and starting ≤ 75 cm from the mineral soil surface, that meets criterion 2 of the [[Gleyic Properties|gleyic properties]] throughout and not having [[Reducing Conditions|reducing conditions]] (2).

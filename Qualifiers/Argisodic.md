#Qualifier
Abbreviation:: as Maingroup:: Sodic

Argisodic (as) (from Latin argilla, white clay): having an [[Argic Horizon|argic horizon]], starting ≤ 100 cm from the mineral soil surface, that has ≥ 15% Na plus Mg and ≥ 6% Na on the exchange complex throughout the argic horizon or within its upper 40 cm, whichever is thinner (2).

Abbreviation:: xa

Xanthic (xa) (from Greek xanthos, yellow): having a ferralic horizon that has in a subhorizon, ≥ 30 cm thick
and starting ≤ 75 cm from the upper limit of the ferralic horizon, in ≥ 90% of its exposed area, a Munsell
colour hue of 7.5YR or yellower, a value of ≥ 4 and a chroma of ≥ 5, all moist.

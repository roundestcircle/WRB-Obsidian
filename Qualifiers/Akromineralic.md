Abbreviation:: km
Maingroup:: [[Mineralic]]

Akromineralic (km) (from Greek akra, top): having mineral material, ≥ 10 cm thick, not consisting
of mulmic material and starting at the soil surface, but the layers of mineral material, not consisting
of mulmic material, above or in between layers of organic material have a combined thickness of
< 20 cm (in Histosols only).

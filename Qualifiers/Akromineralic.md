#Qualifier
Abbreviation:: km Maingroup:: Mineralic

Akromineralic (km) (from Greek akra, top): having [[Mineral Material|mineral material]], ≥ 10 cm thick, not consisting of [[Mulmic Material|mulmic material]] and starting at the soil surface, but the layers of mineral material, not consisting of mulmic material, above or in between layers of [[Organic Material|organic material]] have a combined thickness of < 20 cm (in Histosols only).

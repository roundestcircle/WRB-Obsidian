Abbreviation:: lj
Maingroup:: [[Novic]]

Loaminovic (lj) (from English loam): having a layer, ≥ 5 and < 50 cm thick, that has, single or in
combination, a texture class of loam, sandy loam, clay loam, sandy clay loam or silty clay loam in its
major part, overlying a buried soil that is classified with preference according to the ‘Rules for
naming soils’ (Chapter 2.4).

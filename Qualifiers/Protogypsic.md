#Qualifier
Abbreviation:: qq Maingroup:: Gypsic

Protogypsic (qq) (from Greek proton, first): having a layer with [[Protogypsic Properties|protogypsic properties]] starting ≤ 100 cm from the mineral soil surface and not having a [[Gypsic Horizon|gypsic]] or [[Petrogypsic Horizon|petrogypsic]] horizon starting ≤ 100 cm from the mineral soil surface (2).

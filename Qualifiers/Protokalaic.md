#Qualifier
Abbreviation:: qk Maingroup:: Kalaic

Protokalaic (qk) (from Greek proton, first): having a layer, ≥ 10 cm thick and starting ≤ 90 cm from the soil surface, with ≥ 25% (by volume, weighted average, related to the whole soil) [[Artefacts|artefacts]] (2: Epi-, Endo- and Amphi- only). Lamellic (ll): (from Latin lamella, metal blade): having two or more lamellae, ≥ 0.5 and < 7.5 cm thick, that have one or both of the following:
• higher clay contents than the directly overlying and underlying layers as stated in the diagnostic criteria 2.a of the [[Argic Horizon|argic horizon]], or
• meet the diagnostic criteria 2.b of the argic horizon, with or without other accumulations, and that have a combined thickness of ≥ 5 cm within 50 cm; the uppermost lamella starting ≤ 100 cm from the mineral soil surface (2).

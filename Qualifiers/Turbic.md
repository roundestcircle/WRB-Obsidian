#Qualifier
Abbreviation:: tu Subgroups:: Relictiturbic

Turbic (tu) (from Latin turbare, to disturb): having features of cryogenic alteration (cryoturbation, mixed material, disrupted soil horizons, involutions, organic intrusions, frost heave, separation of coarse from fine materials, cracks, patterned ground etc.) in some layer within 100 cm of the soil surface and above a [[Cryic Horizon|cryic horizon]] or above a seasonally frozen layer (2: only if clearly recognizable as a layer).

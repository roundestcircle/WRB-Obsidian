Abbreviation:: rw
Maingroup:: [[Stagnic]]

Relictistagnic (rw) (from Latin relictus, left back): having a layer, ≥ 25 cm thick and starting ≤ 75 cm
from the mineral soil surface, that has:
• stagnic properties in which the area of oximorphic features is ≥ 10% (weighted average, related to
the fine earth plus oximorphic features of any size and any cementation class) of the layer’s total
area, and
• no reducing conditions (2).

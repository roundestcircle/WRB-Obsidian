Abbreviation:: ga
Subgroups:: [[Hypergarbic]]

Garbic (ga) (from English garbage): having a layer, ≥ 20 cm thick and within 100 cm of the soil surface,
with ≥ 20% (by volume, weighted average, related to the whole soil) artefacts, ≥ 35% of which contain
≥ 20% organic carbon (e.g. organic waste) (in Technosols only) (2).

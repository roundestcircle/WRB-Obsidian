#Qualifier
Abbreviation:: cr

Chromic (cr) (from Greek chroma, colour): having between 25 and 150 cm of the mineral soil surface a layer, ≥ 30 cm thick, that shows evidence of soil formation as defined in criterion 3 of the [[Cambic Horizon|cambic horizon]] and that has, in ≥ 90% of its exposed area, a Munsell colour hue redder than 7.5YR and a chroma of > 4, both moist, and that does not meet the set of diagnostic criteria of the [[Rhodic]] qualifier.

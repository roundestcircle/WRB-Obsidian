#Qualifier
Abbreviation:: ao

Acroxic (ao) (from Latin acer, sharp, and Greek oxys, sour): having within 100 cm of the soil surface one or more layers with a combined thickness of ≥ 30 cm, and with < 2 cmolc kg-1 fine earth exchangeable bases (by 1 M NH4OAc, pH 7) plus exchangeable Al (by 1 M KCl, unbuffered) (in Andosols only) (2).

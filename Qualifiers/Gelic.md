Abbreviation:: ge

Gelic (ge) (from Latin gelare, to freeze):
• having a layer with a soil temperature of < 0 °C for ≥ 2 consecutive years, starting ≤ 200 cm from the
soil surface, and
• not having a cryic horizon starting ≤ 100 cm from the soil surface, and
• not having a cryic horizon starting ≤ 200 cm from the soil surface with evidence of cryogenic alteration
in some layer within 100 cm of the soil surface.

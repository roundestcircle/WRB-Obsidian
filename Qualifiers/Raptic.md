#Qualifier
Abbreviation:: rp

Raptic (rp) (from Latin raptus, broken): having a lithic discontinuity at some depth ≤ 100 cm from the mineral soil surface that is not related to [[Aeolic Material|aeolic]], [[Fluvic Material|fluvic]], [[Solimovic Material|solimovic]] or [[Tephric Material|tephric]] material (1).

#Qualifier
Abbreviation:: sj Maingroup:: Novic

Siltinovic (sj) (from English silt): having a layer, ≥ 5 and < 50 cm thick, that has, single or in combination, a texture class of silt or silt loam in its major part, overlying a buried soil that is classified with preference according to the ‘Rules for naming soils’ (Chapter 2.4). Combinations possible to indicate the deposited material (see Chapter 2.4).

#Qualifier
Abbreviation:: jr Maingroup:: Sideralic

Hypersideralic (jr) (from Greek hyper, over): having within 150 cm of the mineral soil surface a layer that has ≥ 8% clay, has a CEC (by 1 M NH4OAc, pH 7) of < 16 cmolc kg-1 clay and shows evidence of soil formation as defined in criterion 3 of the [[Cambic Horizon|cambic horizon]]; and not having a [[Ferralic Horizon|ferralic horizon]] starting ≤ 150 cm from the mineral soil surface (2).

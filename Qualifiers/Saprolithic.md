#Qualifier
Abbreviation:: sh Maingroup:: Lithic

Saprolithic (sh) (from Greek sapros, rotten, and lithos, stone): having a layer, ≥ 30 cm thick and starting ≤ 150 cm from the mineral soil surface, that has rock structure in ≥ 75% (by volume, related to the whole soil) and a CEC (by 1 M NH4OAc, pH 7) of < 24 cmolc kg-1 clay (2).

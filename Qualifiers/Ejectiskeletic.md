Abbreviation:: jk
Maingroup:: [[Skeletic]]

Ejectiskeletic (jk) (from Latin ejicere, to throw out): having ≥ 40% (by volume, related to the whole
soil) coarse fragments of pyroclastic origin (lapilli, bombs and/or blocks), averaged over a depth of
100 cm from the mineral soil surface or to a limiting layer, whichever is shallower (5).

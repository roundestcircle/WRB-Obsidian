Abbreviation:: la

Laxic (la) (from Latin laxus, slack): having between 25 and 75 cm from the mineral soil surface a mineral
soil layer, ≥ 20 cm thick, that has a bulk density of ≤ 0.9 kg dm-3.
Note: For bulk density, the volume is determined after an undried soil sample has been desorbed at 33
kPa (no prior drying), and afterwards the weight is determined at 105 °C (see Annex 2, Chapter 9.5).

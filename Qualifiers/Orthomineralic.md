#Qualifier
Abbreviation:: oi Maingroup:: Mineralic

Orthomineralic (oi) (from Greek orthos, right): having:
• [[Mineral Material|mineral material]], ≥ 10 cm thick, not consisting of [[Mulmic Material|mulmic material]] and starting at the soil surface, and
• within 100 cm of the soil surface, one or more layers of mineral material, not consisting of mulmic material, with a combined thickness of ≥ 20 cm, above or in between layers of [[Organic Material|organic material]] (in [[Histosols]] only) (2: Epi-, Endo-, Amphi- and Poly- only).

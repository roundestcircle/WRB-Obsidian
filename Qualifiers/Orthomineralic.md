Abbreviation:: oi
Maingroup:: [[Mineralic]]

Orthomineralic (oi) (from Greek orthos, right): having:
• mineral material, ≥ 10 cm thick, not consisting of mulmic material and starting at the soil surface,
and
• within 100 cm of the soil surface, one or more layers of mineral material, not consisting of mulmic
material, with a combined thickness of ≥ 20 cm, above or in between layers of organic material
(in Histosols only) (2: Epi-, Endo-, Amphi- and Poly- only).

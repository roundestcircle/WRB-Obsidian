#Qualifier
Abbreviation:: oa

Oxyaquic (oa) (from Greek oxys, sour, and Latin aqua, water): having a layer, ≥ 25 cm thick and starting ≤ 75 cm from the mineral soil surface, that is saturated with water during a period of ≥ 20 consecutive days; and not having [[Gleyic Properties|gleyic properties]] and not having [[Stagnic Properties|stagnic properties]] in any layer within 100 cm of the mineral soil surface (2).

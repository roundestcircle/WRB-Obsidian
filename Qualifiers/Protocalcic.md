Abbreviation:: qc
Maingroup:: [[Calcic]]

Protocalcic (qc) (from Greek proton, first): having a layer with protocalcic properties starting
≤ 100 cm from the mineral soil surface and not having a calcic or petrocalcic horizon starting
≤ 100 cm from the mineral soil surface (not in Chernozems and Kastanozems, where protocalcic
properties are part of the definition) (2).

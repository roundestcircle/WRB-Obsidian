#Qualifier
Abbreviation:: qc Maingroup:: Calcic

Protocalcic (qc) (from Greek proton, first): having a layer with [[Protocalcic Properties|protocalcic properties]] starting ≤ 100 cm from the mineral soil surface and not having a [[Calcic Horizon|calcic]] or [[Petrocalcic Horizon|petrocalcic]] horizon starting ≤ 100 cm from the mineral soil surface (not in [[Chernozems]] and [[Kastanozems]], where protocalcic properties are part of the definition) (2).

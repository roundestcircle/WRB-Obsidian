#Qualifier
Abbreviation:: pi

Placic (pi) (from Greek plax, flat stone): having a layer, ≥ 0.1 and < 2.5 cm thick and within 100 cm of the mineral soil surface, that is cemented, with a cementation class of at least weakly cemented, by Fe oxides, with or without other cementing agents, and is continuous to the extent that vertical fractures, if present, have an average horizontal spacing of ≥ 10 cm and occupy < 20% (by volume, related to the whole soil) (2: Epi-, Endo- and Amphi- only).

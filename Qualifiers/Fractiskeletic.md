Abbreviation:: fk
Maingroup:: [[Skeletic]]

Fractiskeletic (fk) (from Latin fractus, broken): having ≥ 40% (by volume, related to the whole soil)
coarse fragments plus remnants of a broken-up cemented layer, > 2 mm, averaged over a depth of
100 cm from the mineral soil surface or to a limiting layer, whichever is shallower; and not fulfilling
the set of criteria of the Duric, Fractic, Pisoplinthic and Skeletic qualifier (5).

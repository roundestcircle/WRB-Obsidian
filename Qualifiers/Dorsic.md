#Qualifier
Abbreviation:: ds

Dorsic (ds) (from Latin dorsum, at a lower position):
• in [[Cryosols]], the [[Cryic Horizon|cryic horizon]] starting > 100 cm from the soil surface,
• in [[Ferralsols]] and [[Podzols]], the [[Ferralic Horizon|ferralic]]/[[Spodic Horizon|spodic]] horizon starting > 100 cm from the mineral soil surface.

Abbreviation:: jl

Hyperalic (jl) (from Greek hyper, over, and Latin alumen, alum): having an argic horizon, starting ≤ 100 cm
from the mineral soil surface, that has a silt to clay ratio of < 0.6 and an Al saturation (effective) of
≥ 50%, throughout or to a depth of 50 cm below its upper limit, whichever is thinner (in Alisols only).

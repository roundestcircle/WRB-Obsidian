#Qualifier
Abbreviation:: ca

Calcaric (ca) (from Latin calcarius, containing lime): having [[Calcaric Material|calcaric material]]:
• in a layer, ≥ 30 cm thick and within 100 cm of the mineral soil surface, or
• in the major part between the mineral soil surface and a limiting layer starting < 60 cm from the mineral soil surface; and not having a [[Calcic Horizon|calcic]] or a [[Petrocalcic Horizon|petrocalcic]] horizon starting ≤ 100 cm from the mineral soil surface (2; no subqualifier if a limiting layer starts < 60 cm from the mineral soil surface).

Abbreviation:: qp
Maingroup:: [[Spodic]]

Protospodic (qp) (from Greek proton, first): having a layer, starting ≤ 100 cm from the mineral soil
surface, that has a pHwater of < 4.6 and has an Alox value of ≥ 0.5% that is ≥ 1.5 times that of the
lowest Alox value of all the mineral layers above; and not having a spodic horizon starting ≤ 200 cm
from the mineral soil surface (2).

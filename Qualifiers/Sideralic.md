#Qualifier
Abbreviation:: se Subgroups:: Hypersideralic

Sideralic (se) (from Greek sideros, iron, and Latin alumen, alum): having within 150 cm of the mineral soil surface a layer that has [[Sideralic Properties|sideralic properties]]; and not having a [[Ferralic Horizon|ferralic horizon]] starting ≤ 150 cm from the mineral soil surface (2).

#Qualifier
Abbreviation:: fe Subgroups:: Hyperferritic

Ferritic (fe) (from Latin ferrum, iron): having a layer, ≥ 30 cm thick and starting ≤ 100 cm from the mineral soil surface, with ≥ 10% Fedith and not forming part of a [[Petroplinthic Horizon|petroplinthic]], [[Pisoplinthic Horizon|pisoplinthic]] or [[Plinthic Horizon|plinthic]] horizon (2).

Abbreviation:: uq

Uterquic (uq) (from Latin uterque, both): having a layer:
• with dominant gleyic properties and some parts with stagnic properties, starting ≤ 75 cm from the
mineral soil surface (in Gleysols only) (2),
• with dominant stagnic properties and some parts with gleyic properties, starting ≤ 75 cm from the
mineral soil surface (in Planosols and Stagnosols only) (2).

#Qualifier
Abbreviation:: uq

Uterquic (uq) (from Latin uterque, both): having a layer:
• with dominant [[Gleyic Properties|gleyic properties]] and some parts with [[Stagnic Properties|stagnic properties]], starting ≤ 75 cm from the mineral soil surface (in [[Gleysols]] only) (2),
• with dominant stagnic properties and some parts with gleyic properties, starting ≤ 75 cm from the mineral soil surface (in [[Planosols]] and [[Stagnosols]] only) (2).

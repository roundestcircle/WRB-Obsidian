#Qualifier
Abbreviation:: cy

Cryic (cy) (from Greek kryos, cold, ice):
• having a [[Cryic Horizon|cryic horizon]] starting ≤ 100 cm from the soil surface, or
• having a cryic horizon starting ≤ 200 cm from the soil surface with evidence of cryogenic alteration in some layer ≤ 100 cm from the soil surface (1; Epi- and Endo- only; referring to the upper limit of the cryic horizon).

#Qualifier
Abbreviation:: gp

Gypsiric (gp) (from Greek gypsos, gypsum): having [[Gypsiric Material|gypsiric material]]:
• in a layer, ≥ 30 cm thick and within 100 cm of the mineral soil surface, or
• in the major part between the mineral soil surface and a limiting layer starting < 60 cm from the mineral soil surface; and not having a [[Gypsic Horizon|gypsic]] or [[Petrogypsic Horizon|petrogypsic]] horizon starting ≤ 100 cm from the mineral soil surface (2; no subqualifier if a limiting layer starts < 60 cm from the mineral soil surface).

#Qualifier
Abbreviation:: kh Maingroup:: Histic

Skeletohistic (kh) (from Greek skeletos, dried out): having a [[Histic Horizon|histic horizon]] starting:
• at the soil surface or
• directly below a layer, < 40 cm thick, consisting of [[Mulmic Material|mulmic material]] or
• directly below a layer, < 40 cm thick, consisting of [[Organic Material|organic material]] that is saturated with water for < 30 consecutive days in most years and is not drained; with ≥ 40% (by volume, weighted average, related to the whole soil) coarse fragments.

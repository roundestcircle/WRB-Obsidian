Abbreviation:: kh
Maingroup:: [[Histic]]

Skeletohistic (kh) (from Greek skeletos, dried out): having a histic horizon starting:
• at the soil surface or
• directly below a layer, < 40 cm thick, consisting of mulmic material or
• directly below a layer, < 40 cm thick, consisting of organic material that is saturated with water for
< 30 consecutive days in most years and is not drained;
with ≥ 40% (by volume, weighted average, related to the whole soil) coarse fragments.

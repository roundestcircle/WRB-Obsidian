#Qualifier
Abbreviation:: qg

Protoargic (qg) (from Greek proton, first, and Latin argilla, white clay): having an absolute clay increase of ≥ 4% from one layer to the directly underlying layer, within 100 cm of the mineral soil surface (in [[Arenosols]] only) (2). Protocalcic (qc): see [[Calcic]]. Protospodic (qp): see [[Spodic]]. Protovertic (qv): see [[Vertic]].

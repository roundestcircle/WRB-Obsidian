Abbreviation:: ar

Arenic (ar) (from Latin arena, sand): consisting of mineral material and having, single or in combination, a
texture class of sand or loamy sand:
• in one or more layers with a combined thickness of ≥ 30 cm, occurring within 100 cm of the mineral
soil surface, or
• in the major part between the mineral soil surface and a limiting layer starting > 10 and < 60 cm from
the mineral soil surface
(2; no subqualifier if a limiting layer starts < 60 cm from the mineral soil surface).
Arenicolic (ad) (related to the worm genus Arenicola): having ≥ 50% (by volume, weighted average) of
worm holes, worm casts, or filled animal burrows in a layer, ≥ 20 cm thick and occurring in a tidal area.

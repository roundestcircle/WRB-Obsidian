#Qualifier
Abbreviation:: hi Subgroups:: Skeletohistic

Histic (hi) (from Greek histos, tissue): having a [[Histic Horizon|histic horizon]] starting:
• at the soil surface, or
• directly below a layer, < 40 cm thick, consisting of [[Mulmic Material|mulmic material]], or
• directly below a layer, < 40 cm thick, consisting of [[Organic Material|organic material]] that is saturated with water for < 30 consecutive days in most years and is not drained.

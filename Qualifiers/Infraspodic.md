Abbreviation:: is
Maingroup:: [[Spodic]]

Infraspodic (is) (from Latin infra, below, and Greek spodos, wood ash): having a layer that underlies a soil
classified with preference according to the ‘Rules for naming soils’ (Chapter 2.4) and that meets
diagnostic criteria 3 to 7 of the spodic horizon and fails diagnostic criterion 1 or 2 or both.

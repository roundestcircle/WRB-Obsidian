#Qualifier
Abbreviation:: ju Maingroup:: Duric

Hyperduric (ju) (from Greek hyper, over): having a [[Duric Horizon|duric horizon]] with ≥ 50% (by volume, related to the whole soil) durinodes or remnants of a broken-up [[Petroduric Horizon|petroduric horizon]] starting ≤ 100 cm from the mineral soil surface (2).

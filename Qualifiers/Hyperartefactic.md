#Qualifier
Abbreviation:: ja

Hyperartefactic (ja) (from Greek hyper, over, and Latin ars, art, and factus, made): having ≥ 50% (by volume, weighted average, related to the whole soil) [[Artefacts|artefacts]] within 100 cm of the soil surface or to a limiting layer, whichever is shallower (in [[Technosols]] only). Hypercalcic (jc): see [[Calcic]]. Hypereutric (je): see [[Eutric]]. Hypergypsic (jy): see [[Gypsic]]. Hypernatric (jn): see [[Natric]].

Abbreviation:: mg
Subgroups:: [[Hypermagnesic]]

Magnesic (mg) (from the chemical element magnesium): having an exchangeable Ca to Mg ratio of < 1:
• in a layer, ≥ 30 cm thick and within 100 cm of the mineral soil surface, or
• in the major part between the mineral soil surface and a limiting layer starting < 60 cm from the mineral
soil surface
(2; no subqualifier if a limiting layer starts < 60 cm from the mineral soil surface).

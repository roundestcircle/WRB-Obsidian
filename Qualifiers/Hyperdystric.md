Abbreviation:: jd
Maingroup:: [[Dystric]]

Hyperdystric (jd) (from Greek hyper, over):
• in Histosols, having a pHwater of < 5.5 throughout in the organic material within 100 cm of the soil
surface and < 4.5 in the major part with organic material within 100 cm of the soil surface,
• in other soils, having mineral material, throughout:
➢ from 20 to 100 cm of the mineral soil surface, or
➢ from 20 cm of the mineral soil surface to a limiting layer starting ≥ 50 cm from the mineral soil
surface,
whichever is shallower,
that has exchangeable Al > exchangeable (Ca+Mg+K+Na); and in its major part exchangeable Al
> 4 times the exchangeable (Ca+Mg+K+Na).

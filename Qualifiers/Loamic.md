#Qualifier
Abbreviation:: lo

Loamic (lo) (from English loam): consisting of [[Mineral Material|mineral material]] and having, single or in combination, a texture class of loam, sandy loam, clay loam, sandy clay loam or silty clay loam:
• in one or more layers with a combined thickness of ≥ 30 cm, occurring within 100 cm of the mineral soil surface, or
• in the major part between the mineral soil surface and a limiting layer starting > 10 and < 60 cm from the mineral soil surface (2; no subqualifier if a limiting layer starts < 60 cm from the mineral soil surface).

#Qualifier
Abbreviation:: go Maingroup:: Abruptic

Geoabruptic (go) (from Greek gaia, earth): having an [[Abrupt textural difference|abrupt textural difference]] within 100 cm of the mineral soil surface that is not associated with the upper limit of an [[Argic Horizon|argic]], [[Natric Horizon|natric]] or [[Spodic Horizon|spodic]] horizon (1).

Abbreviation:: az

Arzic (az) (from Turkish arz, land or earth’s crust): saturated by groundwater or flowing water in some layer
within 50 cm of the soil surface during some time in most years and containing ≥ 15% gypsum averaged
over a depth of 100 cm from the soil surface or to a limiting layer, whichever is shallower (in Gypsisols
only).

#Qualifier
Abbreviation:: ma

Mahic (ma) (from Maori mahi, work):
• having a layer, ≥ 10 cm thick and starting ≤ 50 cm from the soil surface, with ≥ 80% (by volume, weighted average, related to the whole soil) [[Artefacts|artefacts]], and
• having < 20% (by volume, weighted average, related to the whole soil) artefacts in the upper 100 cm from the soil surface or to a limiting layer, whichever is shallower. Mawic (mw): (from Kiswahili mawe, stones): having a layer of coarse fragments that, together with overlying organic material, if present, starts at the soil surface and has a thickness of:
• ≥ 10 cm if overlying continuous rock or [[Technic Hard Material|technic hard material]], or
• ≥ 40 cm; and the major part of the interstices between the coarse fragments is filled with [[Organic Material|organic material]] and the remaining interstices, if present, are void (in [[Histosols]] only) (1: Epi- and Endo- only; referring to the upper limit of the layer of coarse fragments).

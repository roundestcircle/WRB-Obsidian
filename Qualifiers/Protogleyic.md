#Qualifier
Abbreviation:: qy Maingroup:: Gleyic

Protogleyic (qy) (from Greek proton, first): having a layer, ≥ 10 cm thick and starting ≤ 75 cm from the mineral soil surface, that has [[Gleyic Properties|gleyic properties]] throughout and [[Reducing Conditions|reducing conditions]] in some parts of every sublayer (2).

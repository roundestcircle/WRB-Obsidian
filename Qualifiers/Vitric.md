Abbreviation:: vi

Vitric (vi) (from Latin vitrum, glass): having within 100 cm of the soil surface:
• in Andosols, one or more layers with vitric properties with a combined thickness of ≥ 30 cm. (2),
• in other soils, one or more layers with andic or vitric properties with a combined thickness of ≥ 30 cm
(in Cambisols ≥ 15 cm), of which ≥ 15 cm (in Cambisols ≥ 7.5 cm) have vitric properties (2).

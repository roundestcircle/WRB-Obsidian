Abbreviation:: jx
Maingroup:: [[Urbic]]

Hyperurbic (jx) (from Greek hyper, over): having a layer, ≥ 50 cm thick and within 100 cm of the
soil surface, with ≥ 35% (by volume, weighted average, related to the whole soil) artefacts
consisting of rubble and refuse of human settlements (in Technosols only) (2).

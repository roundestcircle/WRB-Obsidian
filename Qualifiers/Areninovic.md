#Qualifier
Abbreviation:: aj Maingroup:: Novic

Areninovic (aj) (from Latin arena, sand): having a layer, ≥ 5 and < 50 cm thick, that has, single or in combination, a texture class of sand or loamy sand in its major part, overlying a buried soil that is classified with preference according to the ‘Rules for naming soils’ (Chapter 2.4).

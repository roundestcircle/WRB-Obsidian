#Qualifier
Abbreviation:: jj Maingroup:: Spolic

Hyperspolic (jj) (from Greek hyper, over): having a layer, ≥ 50 cm thick and within 100 cm of the soil surface, with ≥ 35% (by volume, weighted average, related to the whole soil) [[Artefacts|artefacts]] consisting of industrial products (in [[Technosols]] only) (2).

Abbreviation:: kf
Maingroup:: [[Fluvic]]

Akrofluvic (kf) (from Greek akra, top): having fluvic material from the mineral soil surface to a depth
of ≥ 5 cm, but < 25 cm thick. (In addition to the Akrofluvic subqualifier, a soil may also have the
Amphifluvic, the Katofluvic or the Endofluvic subqualifier.)

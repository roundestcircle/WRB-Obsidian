#Qualifier
Abbreviation:: nb Maingroup:: Brunic

Neobrunic (nb) (from Greek neos, new): having a layer, ≥ 15 cm thick and starting ≤ 50 cm from the mineral soil surface, that meets diagnostic criteria 3 and 4 of the [[Cambic Horizon|cambic horizon]] but fails diagnostic criterion 1, does not consist of [[Claric Material|claric material]] and overlies:
• an [[Albic Horizon|albic horizon]] that overlies an [[Argic Horizon|argic]], a [[Natric Horizon|natric]] or a [[Spodic Horizon|spodic horizon]], or
• a layer with [[Retic Properties|retic properties]].

Abbreviation:: nb
Maingroup:: [[Brunic]]

Neobrunic (nb) (from Greek neos, new): having a layer, ≥ 15 cm thick and starting ≤ 50 cm from the
mineral soil surface, that meets diagnostic criteria 3 and 4 of the cambic horizon but fails diagnostic
criterion 1, does not consist of claric material and overlies:
• an albic horizon that overlies an argic, a natric or a spodic horizon, or
• a layer with retic properties.

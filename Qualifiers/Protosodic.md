Abbreviation:: qs
Maingroup:: [[Sodic]]

Protosodic (qs) (from Greek proton, first): having a layer, ≥ 20 cm thick and starting ≤ 100 cm from
the mineral soil surface, that has ≥ 6% Na and < 15% Na plus Mg on the exchange complex; and not
having a natric horizon starting ≤ 100 cm from the soil surface (2).

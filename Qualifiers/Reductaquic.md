#Qualifier
Abbreviation:: ra

Reductaquic (ra) (from Latin reductus, drawn back, and aqua, water): having above a [[Cryic Horizon|cryic horizon]] a layer, ≥ 25 cm thick and starting ≤ 75 cm from the soil surface, that is saturated with water during the thawing period and that has for some time during the year [[Reducing Conditions|reducing conditions]] (in [[Cryosols]] only) (2).

Abbreviation:: il

Isolatic (il) (from Italian isola, island): having, above technic hard material, above a geomembrane or above
a continuous layer of artefacts starting ≤ 100 cm from the soil surface, soil material containing fine earth
without any contact to other soil material containing fine earth (e.g. soils on roofs or in pots).
Isopteric (ip) (related to Isoptera, zoologic order of termites): having a layer, ≥ 30 cm thick and starting at
the mineral soil surface, that is remodelled by termites, has a bulk density ≤ 1.3 kg dm-3 and < 5%
particles ≥ 630 µm (2: Ano- and Panto- only).

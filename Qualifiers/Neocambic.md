#Qualifier
Abbreviation:: nc Maingroup:: Cambic

Neocambic (nc) (from Greek neos, new): having a [[Cambic Horizon|cambic horizon]], not consisting of [[Claric Material|claric material]], starting ≤ 50 cm from the mineral soil surface and overlying:
• an [[Albic Horizon|albic horizon]] that overlies an [[Argic Horizon|argic]], a [[Natric Horizon|natric]] or a [[Spodic Horizon|spodic horizon]], or
• a layer with [[Retic Properties|retic properties]].

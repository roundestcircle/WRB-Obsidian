#Qualifier
Abbreviation:: es

Eutrosilic (es) (from Greek eu, good, and trophae, food, and Latin silex, silicon-containing material): having within 100 cm of the soil surface one or more layers with a combined thickness of ≥ 30 cm with [[Andic Properties|andic properties]] and a sum of exchangeable bases (by 1 M NH4OAc, pH 7) of ≥ 15 cmolc kg-1 fine earth (in [[Andosols]] only) (2).
